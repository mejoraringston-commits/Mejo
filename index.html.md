## 5. DESIGN SYSTEM
- **Palette:** dark navy/charcoal (#0F172A-range) for hero and dark bands, white/off-white (#F8FAFC) for content sections, **one** professional accent (a teal or confident blue), subtle 1px borders, soft shadows. Define everything as CSS variables/Tailwind tokens.
- **Type:** Inter, Manrope or Plus Jakarta Sans. Big bold H1, clear H2/H3 scale, comfortable body text (16–18px, ~1.6 line-height), compact uppercase labels for KPIs.
- **Cards:** modest radius (8–12px), thin borders, consistent padding. No cartoonish rounding.
- **Layout:** 12-column grid, generous whitespace, max content width ~1200px, alternating light and dark section backgrounds for rhythm.
- **Imagery:** inline SVG or chart components only (Recharts or Chart.js, or hand-built SVG). No stock photos.
- **Icons:** Lucide or similar, consistent stroke weight.

## 6. MOTION
Subtle and fast: section fade-in on scroll, KPI count-up, progressive chart reveal, card hover lift, nav transitions, smooth scroll. Everything must respect `prefers-reduced-motion` (disable or simplify).
